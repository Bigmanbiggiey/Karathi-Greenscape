🌿 Karathi Greenscape Company

About Us

Karathi Greenscape Company is a trusted name in landscaping, plant nurseries, and ornamental gardening solutions. Based in Kitengela and Limuru, we are dedicated to nurturing nature and beautifying spaces through high-quality plants, trees, and garden accessories.

With a passion for sustainability and over a decade of experience, we pride ourselves in providing grafted fruits, ornamental and exotic trees, flowers, palms, and durable pots to meet both domestic and commercial landscaping needs. Our mission is to make your environment greener, healthier, and more beautiful.
